## Gene content and important observations

The complete chloroplast genome of _Nymphaea nouchali_ is 159,978 bp long and has a GC content of 39.14%. The genome contains 130 annotated genes, including 85 protein-coding genes, 37 tRNA genes, and 8 rRNA genes. It has the typical LSC–IR–SSC–IR structure, with an LSC region of 90,001 bp, an SSC region of 19,603 bp, and two IR regions of 25,187 bp each. Some genes occur in two copies because they are located in the inverted-repeat regions. The genes in the genome are involved in important functions such as photosynthesis, ATP production, RNA processing, and protein synthesis. The genome has a typical chloroplast structure and contains the main genes needed for chloroplast functions.
